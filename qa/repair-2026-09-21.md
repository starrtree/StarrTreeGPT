## Findings and repair
- Outer orbit container and mobile picker captured pointerdown unconditionally. That retargets subsequent clicks away from the original canvas/button. Capture is now delayed until movement exceeds 8 pixels.
- Drag flags were reset using a zero-delay timer on release. They now persist until the next pointerdown; cancelled gestures also suppress clicks. Both axes contribute to drag detection.
- R3F orb and central figure handlers reject movement greater than 8 pixels; ancestor capture-phase handlers classify gestures before R3F processes them.
- Existing selectPlanet -> selectedId -> selectedWorld/activeCard -> planet-interface/GSAP transition preserved.
- Mobile previously omitted all OrbNode instances even when a category was selected. It now renders only the selected existing model in the expanded view.
- Back to System previously closed on pointerdown and click. It now closes only on click.
- Model URLs, materials, textures, lights, intro timings, camera and orbit transforms are unchanged relative to live version 29.
